# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales transaction data to identify revenue trends, profitable products, regional performance, and business opportunities.

## Business Questions

- What are the total sales and profit?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which region performs best?
- Which category contributes the most sales?
- What is the average order value?
- Where are there opportunities to improve performance?

## Tools Used

- Excel
- SQL
- Power BI
- Data Analysis
- Business Analytics

## Dataset

The practice dataset contains 500 sales transactions with fields including:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer
- City
- Region
- Product
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Discount
- Sales
- Profit

## Key Findings

- Laptop is the top-selling product by revenue.
- North is the strongest region by sales.
- Electronics is the dominant revenue category.
- Laptop contributes approximately 30.6% of total sales.
- The analysis can help management compare regional performance and identify growth opportunities.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate why the North region is outperforming other regions.
2. Protect and grow the Laptop product category because of its strong revenue contribution.
3. Analyze discount levels against profit to avoid unnecessary margin loss.
4. Identify products and regions with weaker performance and create targeted sales strategies.
